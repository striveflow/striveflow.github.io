Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Information We Collect

StriveFlow collects fitness activity data recorded during workouts and device identifiers. Bluetooth sensor data is processed locally on your device.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide and improve StriveFlow. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Storage

Workout data is stored locally using AsyncStorage. If you create an account, data syncs to our secure Supabase database. You may delete your account and all data at any time from Settings.

Third-Party Services

If you connect Strava, activity data you upload is governed by Strava's own privacy policy. We only write activity data to Strava — we do not read or store any Strava data within StriveFlow.

Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your StriveFlow account and all associated data at any time, in either of these ways:

  1. In the app, go to Settings → Account and tap Delete Account.
  2. Email us at striveflowapp@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request," sent from the email address associated with your account.

Once we receive your request, we will permanently delete your account and all associated data — including your profile, activities, routes, photos, and social posts — within 30 days.
